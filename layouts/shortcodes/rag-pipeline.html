{{- /* The RAGWorkflow stage sequence, as implemented in magicaf-core rag/mod.rs. Optional stages are drawn dashed. */ -}}
  1. Guardprompt_guardReject or sanitize prompt-injection attempts in the question.
  2. EmbedEmbeddingServiceTurn the question into a vector with embed_single.
  3. RetrieveVectorStoreSearch the collection for the top_k nearest passages, then drop any below min_score.
  4. RerankRerankerReorder passages with a cross-encoder, or filter by score.
  5. FormatEvidenceFormatterRender passages into the evidence block the model will read.
  6. PromptPromptBuilderCombine evidence and question into the user message.
  7. GenerateLlmServiceCall the model with the prompt and GenerationConfig.
  8. Checkresponse_guardValidate the raw reply's size and shape.
  9. ParseResultParser<T>Turn the reply into your result type.
  10. Auditaudit_loggerEmit an audit event, success or failure, and record rag_pipeline_duration_seconds.
